Code of Conduct

The following is the code of conduct is valid for any type of social interaction in My Profit Land, country shouts, world shouts, forums, company names, newspapers, in game chat, private messages.
To use any of these interactive channels the player has to agree to follow the rules of the code of conduct.
Failure to follow them will result in deleted messages, revoked the ability to post or send messages, temporary and permanent account bans. The enforcement of these rules is done by the moderators, they do so based on their best effort to keep things civil between all. The moderator has the final word regarding any miss conduct of a player.

What cannot be done

  • Clone accusations. Report them directly to our support. https://myprofitland.com/contact.php
  • Name shaming and personal threats.
  • Instigating others against moderators or administrators.
  • Publicly confronting moderators or administrators about decisions regarding the moderation of the game.
  • Messages that aim to lower the reputation of the game.
  • Negative messages that incite players not to invest in the game.
  • No advertising of other online games or money earning sites. With our without referral links
  • When posting in public countries local shouts, only the native language of that country can be used or english.
  • Begging.
  • Inciting others to drop their account and create a new one under a different recruiter.
