📖 About The Game

Glory Verse (GV) is an online strategy browser game focused on economic simulation, resource management, and warfare. Players can open and operate various companies that form an interconnected supply chain — from gathering raw materials to producing high-tech military equipment.

The game emphasizes energy management, work hours, licenses, and strategic resource distribution.
🏭 Company System

Companies in GV are divided into 5 major categories:
1. Utilities

The foundation of all production.

    Solar Panel Farm & Water Well Drilling - Produces Electricity & Water (Storage 400, 500 uses license, autonomous)

2. Gathering & Extraction

Raw material producers.

    Gathering: Cereal Farm, Coffee Plantation, Cotton Plantation, Herbs Plantation, Vegetables Farm, Vineyard, Wood Cutter
    Extraction: Coal Mine, Iron Mine, Oil Extraction, Sand Pit, Stone Quarry, Uranium Mine
    Special Note: 5 Uranium regions change randomly every month

3. Services & Hand Working

First-level processing.

    Examples: Mill, Bakery, Cow Farm, Foundry, Glass Factory, Paper Mill, Recycling Center

4. Engineering

Advanced manufacturing.

    Examples: Laboratory, Parts Factory, Plastics Factory, Refinery, Fabrics Factory, Attack/Defense Weapons Factory, Military Factory, Vehicles Factory

5. Consumer & Guest Servicing

End products that restore energy or are used for war/travel.

    Energy: Brewery (Beer), Cheese Shop, Restaurant (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner), Winery (Wine), Milk & Coffee Shop
    Infrastructure: House Construction (Small House, Nice Apartment, Mansion)
    Military: Military Factory (Chemical Bomb, Nuclear Bomb, Ballistic Missile, Heavy Tank, Bomber Jet, Gas Mask)
    Travel: Vehicles Factory (Citroën C4, Private Jet), Filling Station (Diesel, Kerosene)
    Knowledge & Bureaucracy: Knowledge Academy (Books for Economic Score), Bank (Receipts), Notary (Governmental, Licensing, Military Forms), Newspaper

⚙️ Core Mechanics

Each company has:

    Input: Raw material (e.g., Wheat 1.0)
    Output & Storage: (e.g., Storage 100)
    Cost: Electricity & Water (e.g., Electricity 0.2, Water 0.2)
    Work Hours: (e.g., 0.5 work hours)
    License: Limited uses (e.g., license for 1000 uses)

Example Chain:
Cereal Farm (Wheat) -> Mill (Wheat Flour) -> Bakery (Bread) -> Restaurant (Breakfast)
