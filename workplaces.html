About Productivity

The offered wage refers to a player with 100% productivity, same happens with the work bonus paid by your country. If for example you only have 50% final productivity then you will only get paid half of what you could.

You can increase productivity in many ways, every time you work you earn economic score, you also learn a skill when you work, these will influence your productivity, the more you work the better you will be overtime. When you consume products that increase your energy, you are also increasing your productivity.

Productivity is based on the formula PROD=(E*0.45)+(ES*0.30)+(SKILL*0.25)+CountryProdBonus+OrganizationProdBonus, where E=Energy, ES=economic score and SKILL=your relevant skill level. Your country productivity bonus is +0%. Your organization productivity bonus is +0%. Economic score drops daily by 1 point and increases after every worked shift by 1 point.
