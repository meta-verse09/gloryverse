About Productivity

The offered wage is for a player with 100% productivity, and so is the work bonus paid by your country. With 50% productivity you get half of what you could.

Working earns economic score and trains a skill, and both raise your productivity. Products that increase energy raise it too.

PROD = (E×0.45) + (ES×0.30) + (SKILL×0.25) + country bonus + organization bonus, where E is energy, ES is economic score and SKILL is your relevant skill. Your country bonus is +0% and your organization bonus is +0%. Economic score drops by 1 point daily and rises by 1 point after every worked shift.
