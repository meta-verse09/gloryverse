Abandoned Recruits

When a player is banned, his recruits will not go to his master, instead they are abandoned and for a period of time they will be linked to a default account (admin). If someone has a real interest in buying one of these recruits, an auction can be started. Auctions last for atleast 24h, bids are made with gold, if a user bids in the last 3 minutes before the auction ends, the term of the auction will be prolonged with another 3 minutes. When you bid, you are withdrawn the sum from your account. If someone outbids you, the sum is given back to you. The new recruit will come with a 24h lock. Auctions start at 2 Gold. All gold from the winning bid will be deposited in the War Fund.
