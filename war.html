War Sponsorship

If you wish to incentivize one of the sides of this war to do damage, you can set a prize money for defenders and/or attackers. If the side you choose to sponsor wins, all the winners from that winning side will get a cut of that prize based on how much damage they did. If the side you sponsored loses, the funds are returned to you.
